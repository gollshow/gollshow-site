  Seoul, 1988 — a city preparing for the Olympics. In the Guro industrial district, Haesu (20), a garment factory worker, endures each day with her head bowed beneath the roar of sewing machines. After sending money home for her sick mother and her siblings' tuition, nothing remains. Her only warmth is Yunhee (24), who works the sewing floor by day and the U.S. military camptown bars by night, saving for her escape to America. Their love can only breathe inside a cramped rooftop room.

  One day, Haesu finds a painting in the factory corridor: two figures entwined on a floor of gold — the single crack of color in a gray world. But the moment their love is witnessed, words become rumor and spread through the factory. In a place where noise devours speech, where language breeds misunderstanding, where rumor becomes violence, Haesu runs for the first time in her life.
